### Cognitive load
Cognitive load is the amount of think you are required to memorise one thing.
The higher the cognitive load, the higher encoding until it overwhelms you.


Repetition has diminishing returns. The first time = very effective, second time = not as effective... Better results focusing on encoding

[[Daily/Habits/Reading.md|Reading]] has 2 stages: Consumption period, and Digestion period
Consumption and digestion must be balanced otherwise you will "overeat" and forget
[[Daily/Habits/Reading.md|Reading]] can be broken down into these categories

P: Procedural - practice
A: Analogous (information with any related or prior knowledge) - make analogy and critique (is this a good analogy?)
C: Conceptual - mapping
E: Evidence - store and rehearse
R: Reference (very specific information that is not very important) - store and rehearse

12 rules of encoding:

1 + 2: If information is not relevant, you will not remember - make information relevant = good
3: Do not consume too much information at once, try to consume and digest constantly because more information = next idea easier to learn so if you digest prev idea next idea = easier
4: Try to simplify everything
5: Compare everything
6: Connect everything
7: Group everything
