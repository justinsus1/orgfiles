A [[Volume]] is a measure of the three-dimensional [[space]] occupied by a substance or object.

In mathematics and geometry, volume is the amount of [[space]] that a three-dimensional object occupies. It is typically measured in cubic units (e.g., cubic meters, cubic centimeters, cubic inches).

For example, the [[Volume]] of a cube is calculated by multiplying its length, width, and height:
$$V = l \times w \times h$$
