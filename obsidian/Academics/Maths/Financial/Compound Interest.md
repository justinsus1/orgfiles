#Maths/Finacial
Compound interest is the interest calculated on accumulated interest

The [[Academics/Formula.md|formula]] for compound interest is:
$F=p(1+i)^n$
Where
	p is principle - the original amount
	i is the interest rate
	n is the duration - in years
