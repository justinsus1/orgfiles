#Maths/Finacial 
Simple interest is the interest calculated only on the initial principal amount. It is a straightforward method used to calculate the interest on a loan or an investment over a specific period.

[[Academics/Formula.md|Formula]] for simple interest is:
$I = pin$
Where 
	p is principle - the initial amount
	i is flat interest - interest rate
	n is duration of interest in years
