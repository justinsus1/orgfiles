#Maths/Algebra/Factorisation

## Core idea

##### Factorisation of $x^2 + bx + c$:
$x^2 + bx + c$ is $(x+p)(x+q)$ 
where $p+q=b$
and $pq=c$

#####  Factorisation of $ax^2 + bx + c$:
is done through "splitting the middle term"
where $bx$ is split into $(px + qx)$
where $p+q = b$
and $pq=ac$

For example:
$$ 
\begin{aligned}
&= 8x^2 + 22x + 15 \\
&= 8x^2 + 12x + 10x + 15 \\
&= (8x^2 + 12x) + (10x + 15) \\
&= 4x(2x + 3) + 5(2x + 3) \\
&= (4x + 5)(2x+3)
\end {aligned}
$$
