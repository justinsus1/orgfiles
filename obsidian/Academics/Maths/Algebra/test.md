# Factorisation

## Quadratics

### x2+bx+cx^2+bx+c

x2+bx+c=(x+p)(x+q)\boxed{x^2+bx+c=(x+p)(x+q)}

Find p,qp,q using:

p+q=bpq=c\boxed{p+q=b} \qquad \boxed{pq=c}

**Why?**

(x+p)(x+q)=x2+(p+q)x+pq(x+p)(x+q) =x^2+(p+q)x+pq

So:

p+q→bpq→cp+q\rightarrow b \qquad pq\rightarrow c

---

### ax2+bx+cax^2+bx+c

**Goal:** split the middle term so that grouping works.

Find p,qp,q:

p+q=bpq=ac\boxed{p+q=b} \qquad \boxed{pq=ac}

Then:

bx→px+qxbx\rightarrow px+qx

**Process**

ax2+bx+cax^2+bx+c ↓\downarrow ax2+px+qx+cax^2+px+qx+c ↓\downarrow (ax2+px)+(qx+c)(ax^2+px)+(qx+c) ↓\downarrow

factor each group

↓\downarrow

common binomial

---

## Example

### Factorise 8x2+22x+158x^2+22x+15

**Find p,qp,q:**

p+q=22p+q=22 pq=8(15)=120pq=8(15)=120 p=12, q=10\boxed{p=12,\ q=10}

**Split:**

8x2+22x+158x^2+22x+15 =8x2+12x+10x+15=8x^2+12x+10x+15

**Group:**

=(8x2+12x)+(10x+15)=(8x^2+12x)+(10x+15)

**Factor:**

=4x(2x+3)+5(2x+3)=4x(2x+3)+5(2x+3)

**Final:**

(4x+5)(2x+3)\boxed{(4x+5)(2x+3)}
