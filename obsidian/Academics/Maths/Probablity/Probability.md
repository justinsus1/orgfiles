#Maths/Probability
## Probability Notation
[[Probability]] of event e is P(e)
Complement of event e is P(e') = 1 - P(e)
Sample Space is all possible outcomes
If an experiment includes two operations you can use a 2 dimensional table/grid
$P(a\cup b) = P(a) + P(b) - P(a \cap b)$
$P(a\cap b) = P(a) \times P(b)$

## Experimental Probability
Frequency is the number of times an even is observed
Relative frequency is frequency divided by total trials, it is also known as the experimental probability
