#Maths/NumberProperties 

## Concept
HCF stands for highest common factor, meaning the largest number that divides into 2 other particular numbers exactly

## Methods
### Factor Tree
Utilise a [[Factor Tree]] for the numbers x and y
Take all the prime factors that x and y share and pick the ones with the lowest exponent
Multiply all the numbers you just picked out

E.g. 24 and 60
24 = 2^3 * 3
60 = 2^2 * 3 * 5
HCF = 2^2 * 3

### IDK
The quickest way to find the HCF of a large number is dividing the larger number by the smaller number and take the smaller number and divide it by the remainder, do this until you reach remainder 0, the last remainder before remainder 0 is the HCF

E.g. 1131, and 1218
$$ \begin{aligned} {1218 \over 1131} = 1 r 87 \\ 1131/87 &= 0 \\ \therefore HCF &= 87\end{aligned} $$
