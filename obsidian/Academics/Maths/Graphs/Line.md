#Maths/Coordinates

A line can be described in 2 ways:

### Gradient intercept form
Gradient intercept form is $y = mx+c$
where m describes the [[Gradient]], and c describes the [[y-intercept]]

### General form
General form is $ax + by = d$
where a, b, and d are constants

- Make sure all coefficients, and constants are integers

This can be transmuted from the gradient intercept form.
E.g.
$$
\begin{aligned}
y = {4 \over 3} x - 4\\
3y = 4x-12\\
3y-4x=-12\\
4x-3y = 12\\
\end{aligned}
$$
