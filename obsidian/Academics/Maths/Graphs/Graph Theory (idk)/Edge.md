- When 2 [[Vertices]] are related you join them by a [[Line]] taking any form, this [[Line]] is known as an [[Edge]]
- Every [[Edge]] must have [[Vertices]] at both ends
![[edge.excalidraw]]

- If an [[Edge]] has the same vertex at both ends this is known as a loop
![[loopEdge.excalidraw|200]]

- Edges are allowed to cross, but they don't "meet" unless they share a vertex
- If [[Vertices]] are joined by 2 or more edges, you can refer to it as a double [[Edge]] or triple [[Edge]]
