
> [!note] Definition
> Particles in gases have higher thermal energy than liquids, they do not have molecular bonds causing them to spread out from eachother.

> [!note] Properties
> Mass: Yes
> Volume: Yes
> Definite shape: No
> Spread out to take shape of container: Yes
> Compressible: Yes
> Energy: High
> Attraction Force: Low
> Arrangement of Particles: Freely moving
