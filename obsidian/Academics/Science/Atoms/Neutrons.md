#Science 

A neutron is a neutrally charged subatomic particle found in the atomic [[Nucleus-physics]], made of one up quark and two down quarks. It helps stabilise the [[Nucleus-physics]] by counteracting proton repulsion, determines an element's [[isotope]]. Free neutrons are unstable, decaying in about 10 minutes.
