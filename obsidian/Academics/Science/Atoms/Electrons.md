#Science 

An [[Electrons]] is a stable, negatively charged elementary [[particle]] that orbits the [[Nucleus-physics]] of an atom. It carries a charge of −1 and has negligible mass compared to [[Protons]] and [[Neutrons]].

In [[Atoms]], [[Electrons]] occupy the space around the [[Nucleus-physics]] in regions called orbitals (or electron clouds).
