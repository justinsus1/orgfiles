#Science 

A [[Nucleus-physics]] is the dense, central part of [[Atoms]] where [Protons](obsidian://open?file=Science%2FAtoms%2FProtons.md) and [Neutrons](obsidian://open?file=Science%2FAtoms%2FNeutrons.md) are found. It is the core of the atom, and the number of [Protons](obsidian://open?file=Science%2FAtoms%2FProtons.md) in the [Nucleus](obsidian://open?file=Science%2FAtoms%2FNucleus.md) defines the element. The nucleus contains all the mass of the atom. The [[Nucleus-physics]] is held together by the strong nuclear [[Force]] of [[Neutrons]].
