#Science 

[[Protons]] are positively charged subatomic particle found in the [[Nucleus-physics]] of an atom, made of two up quarks and one down quark. It carries a charge of +1, and its count in the [[Nucleus-physics]] defines what element an atom is — for example, any atom with 6 protons is carbon. [[Protons]] are stable (they don't decay under normal conditions) and, together with [[Neutrons]], make up virtually all of an atom's [[mass]]
