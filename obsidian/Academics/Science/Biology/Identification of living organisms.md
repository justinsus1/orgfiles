#Science/Biology
## Utilise MRS C GREN

Movement: Can move
Respiration: Conversion of fuel to chemical energy
Sensitivity: Responds to stimulus
Control: Can maintain internal conditions
Growth: Can grow
Reproduction: Can reproduce
Excretion: Gets rid of waste
Nutrition: Intaking food to survive
