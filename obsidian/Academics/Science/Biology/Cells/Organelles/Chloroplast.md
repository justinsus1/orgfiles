#Science/Biology/Organelles
> [!note] Definition
> The Chloroplast is an organelle contained with plants that perform [[Photosynthesis]]

Chloroplasts contain chlorophyll - captures light -> fuel for [[mitochondria]]


### Photosynthesis

The equation for photosynthesis is:
Carbon Dioxide + Water + Light-> Glucose + Oxygen
