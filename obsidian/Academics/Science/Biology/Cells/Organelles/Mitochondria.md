> [!note]- Definition
> The mitochondria is often known as the powerhouse of the cell - it is responsible for cellular respiration, converting food into usable energy for the cell

## Respiration

### Animalia

Aerobic respiration for animalia is
Glucose + O2 (dioxide - 2 oxygen) -> CO2 (carbon dioxide) + Water + ATP (chemical energy)

Anaerobic respiration for animalia is
Glucose -> ATP (chemical energy) + lactic acid (makes muscles stiffness)
- O2 (dioxide - 2 oxygen) helps break down lactic acid into more ATP, CO2 and Water
