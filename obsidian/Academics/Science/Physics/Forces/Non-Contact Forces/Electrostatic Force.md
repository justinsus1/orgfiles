#Science/Physics/Forces 

Electrostatic [[force]] refers to the [[force]] between stationary electric charges.

### Charges
- When there is more [[Electrons]] than [[Protons]] an atom is negatively charged
- When there is more [[Protons]] than [[Electrons]] an atom is positively charged
- When the amount of [[Electrons]] to [[Protons]] are equal the atom is neutrally charged
