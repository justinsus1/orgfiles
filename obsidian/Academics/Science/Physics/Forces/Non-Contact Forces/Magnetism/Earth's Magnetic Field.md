
- The Earth is surrounded by a magnetic field
- The Earth is the only rocky planet in our solar system to have a magnetic field

The movement of liquid iron in Earth's outer core creates the magnetic field

The poles can reverse (last happened 780,000 years ago)

### Importance:
The magnetic fields shield Earth from harmful radiation and particles (mainly from the Sun). Without the magnetic field radiation could destroy Earth's atmosphere.

### Auroras:
![An image of an aurora occuring|560](aurora.jpg)
Auroras are caused by charged [[particles]] deflecting off of the [[Earth's Magnetic Field]]. When they deflect they deflect towards the magnetic poles and hit the atmosphere, this results in vibrant rays/curtains across the sky.

### Animals utilising the magnetic field:
Many different animals use the Earth's magnetic field to navigate, much like how humans use compasses. However scientists have yet to figure out how they achieve this
