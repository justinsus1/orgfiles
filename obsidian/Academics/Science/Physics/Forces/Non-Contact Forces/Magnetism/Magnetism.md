#Science/Physics/Forces

The 3 main natural magnetic materials are [[Iron]], [[Cobalt]], and [[Nickel]]
When the [[electric]] fields of [[Electrons]] align they become magnetic

### Electromagnetism
When a current is ran through a material, it forces the alignment of [[Electrons]] to allow [[electricity]]. However since the [[Electrons]] become aligned, it becomes temporarily magnetic.

| :------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Permanent magnets are permanent             | Magnetic                                                           |
| Permanent magnets don't require electrictiy | Attract naturally magnetic materials like Iron, Cobalt, and Nickel |
| Electromagnet strength can be increased     |                                                                    |
