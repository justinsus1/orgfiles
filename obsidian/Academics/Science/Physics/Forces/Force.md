#Science/Physics/Forces 


## Main Concept
In physics, a force is an action that results in a change to velocity, shape, or to resist other forces.


### Contact Forces
- [[Friction]]
- [[Normal Force]]
### Non-Contact Forces
- [[Magnetism]]
- [[Gravity]]
- [[Electrostatic Force]]


### Effects on Motion
Balanced Forces
- No change

Unbalanced Forces
- Change in velocity or direction
