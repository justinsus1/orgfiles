#Science/Physics/Forces 

A contact [[Force]] that opposes relative [[motion]] between [[surfaces]] in contact.

![microscopic image of surfaces](frictionSurfaces.png)
Even smooth surfaces are rough
When the two surfaces catch it creates friction

### Types of friction
- Static Friction
- [[Kinetic]] (sliding) Friction
- Rolling Friction
- [[Fluid]] Friction

### Factors affecting friction
- Surface roughness
- [[Force]] pressing surfaces together
- Type of material
- Lubrication
