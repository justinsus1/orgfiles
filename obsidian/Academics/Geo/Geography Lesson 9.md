Imagine you live in Australia thousand of years ago. Write a legend that explains how the 3 sisters were created

Once there were 3 sisters who were great mountain warriors named Murkh,  Kachra, and Lalchi. They were mischievous gods who liked to play tricks on people and wreak havoc. One day the sisters' elder, Gyani decided to go out hunting. Gyani caught a great fish as large as a mountain and the whole clan had a giant feast. However, the 3 sisters decided to play a trick and stole the great fish for themselves, they then and replaced it with dirt. In Gyani's rage he punished them by immobilising them permanently.
