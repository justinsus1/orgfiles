
### Life and Death

Oxymoron "Doctor Death" (Page 1):
- Doctor is associated with life, so "Doctor Death" explores the duality of life and death
 - Evokes curious attitude towards death - constructs foundation for Almond to build upon throughout the story

Characterisation "I thought he was Dead" (Page 1):
- "thought" creates ambiguity around the line between death and life
- Illustrates severity of Skellig's illness and how he is on the brink of death
- Evokes a desperate and hopeless atmosphere as Skellig is dying
- Creates a turning point for Almond to pivot off of

Physical Metaphor "Then he turned to face the daylight" (Page 81):
- Illustrates Skellig's transformation in a literal way turning towards the "daylight" representing life and hope
- Synthesises with "I thought he was dead" to illustrate rebirth

### Creativity

Metaphor "You have to see it with your minds eye" (Page 3)
- Establishes the importance of creativity as a key theme throughout the book
- Implies that creativity shifts your perception of reality

Intertexual reference "He said we were surrounded by angels and spirits. We must just open our eyes a little wider, look a little harder" (Page 122):
- Illustrates an expanded perception beyond reality through creativity
- Focuses on imagination

Metaphor "The mind need to be opened out into the world, not shut inside of a gloomy classroom" (Page 47)
- Creates contrast between openness and classroom
- Suggest freedom is achieved not through a classroom

### Nurture and Care

Characterisation "You can't just sit like that waiting to die" (Page 45):
- Illustrates how Michael wants to help Skellig throught "can't"
- Evokes a grim atmosphere toward Skellig
- Michael's care will lead to action

Metaphor "Then he turned to face the daylight" (Page 81):
- "turned" illustrates delibrate action and how through Michael and Mina's persistence Skellig finally decided to change
- "face" has a connotation of bravery as it can be used to describe accepting a challenge to a fight, illustrates how nurture and care can heal someone

Visual imagery "covered in cracked and crooked feathers" (Page 89):
- How change can be long and painful at first even with the love and care of other
