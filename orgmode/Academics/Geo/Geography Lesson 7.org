1. The value of landscape is within the aesthetic, economic, and
   cultural value.

2. 
   1. The aesthetic value of a landscape is closely related to the
      landscape, as it decides the cultural, economic, and spiritual
      value as well, different landscapes can create different
      atmospheres through the aesthetic. Aesthetic landscapes can
      construct a happy, free, and fulfillng, or sad, and gloomy moods.
      This could decide the cultural significance of the landscape, as a
      gloomy aesthetic to a certain landscape could increase the chances
      of it being a burial site. A happy, free, and fulfilling aesthetic
      could increase the chances of it becoming a tourist attraction.

   2. The cultural value of a landscape is linked to the importance of a
      landscape, the cultural significance could add a deeper meaning,
      thus increasing the beauty of it, landscapes can also shape the
      cultures that were built around it.

   3. The spiritual value is closely linked to the cultural value, a
      spiritual landscape can create cultures, and myths surrounding
      landscapes more emotional layers.

   4. The economical value of a landscape is closely related to every
      other value. If a landscape attracts enough tourists, it could
      provide money for the country, possibly leading to better
      education, and health care.

   5. The same landscape can be valued for different reasons. The
      government may value a landscape for the economical value
      provided, the people may value a landmark for its cultural value,
      and an artist may value a landscape for its aesthetic value

3. Great wall of China is a valued, culturally, aesthetically, and
   economically. The cultural value of the Great wall of China lies in
   its history, it was built throughout 2o00~ years, and used to defend
   against various enemies. This rich history gives it an unique
   cultural value, the magnificent construction value also gives it a
   rustic one of a kind aesthetic. The cultural, and aesthetic values
   sythesise and make it a great tourist attraction, thus giving it
   economical values.
