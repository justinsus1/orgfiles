In the graph to left, the exact same thing is happening, however it is
reversed What are heating and cooling curves?
[Obsidian embed requires review: ![[heating&coolingCurve.excalidraw]]]::Graphs showing how temperature changes as a
substance is heated or cooled, including plateaus where a change of
state occurs.

What happens to temperature during a change of state on a heating or
cooling curve?::The temperature temporarily plateaus (stays constant).

Why does temperature plateau during a change of state?::The energy being
transferred is used to change the state of the substance rather than
increase or decrease its temperature.

What happens at point B on a heating curve? [Obsidian embed requires review: ![[heating&coolingCurve.excalidraw]]]::The
substance changes from a solid to a liquid (melting), so the temperature
temporarily remains constant.

Where does the energy go during melting?::The absorbed energy is used to
weaken the forces of attraction between particles and change the
substance from solid to liquid.

What happens to the particles' forces of attraction when a solid
melts?::They are weakened as energy is absorbed.

What is the difference between a heating curve and a cooling curve?::A
heating curve shows a substance absorbing energy and generally
increasing in temperature, while a cooling curve shows a substance
releasing energy and generally decreasing in temperature.

What happens during a plateau on a cooling curve?::The substance is
undergoing a change of state while releasing energy, so its temperature
remains constant temporarily.

What is melting?::The change of state from solid to liquid.

What is the key feature of a phase-change plateau?::Temperature remains
constant while the substance changes state.
