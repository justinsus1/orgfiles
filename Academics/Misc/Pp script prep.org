Scene 2:

Narrator: It was midday when the Wolf arrived at Grandma's house.

Shift scenes to Grandma alone in the house.

Spiderwebs and rotten wood decorate the room and Grandma's tummy
rumbles. Wolf waits outside the door and knocks. He carries the basket
Little Red carried

Grandma: Yes? Come in.

Wolf enters

Wolf: I am truly sorry for your loss but your little girl walked off.
Apparently she had poor reception. 

Narrator: Grandma was clearly confused at what the Wolf was saying as
she had been suffering from dementia. She fumbled around the room before
she happened to remember that Little Red was supposed to send her food
that exact day.

Grandma [dementia]: Who is that? Oh! Little Red! Nice to see you!

Narrator: Grandma had extremely poor vision or she was ignorant or...
was both.

Wolf: Errr...Yes?

Grandma: Wonderful! Now. Come over...

Wolf edges closer.

Grandma: Closer.

Wolf edges closer.

Grandma: I SAID CLOSER.

/Wolf hurries over /

Grandma: Good good. Er... you seem to be wearing a very large fur
coat.\\
Wolf: I... yeah! Uhhh yeah, it is very cold outside!

Narrator: The wolf was indeed not wearing a large fur coat nor was it
cold.

Grandma: And what big teeth. Did your adult teeth come in?

Wolf: Yeah. 

Narrator: The Wolf did not have new teeth.

Wolf: I AM HERE TO EAT YOU... I mean with you.

Wolf picks up little red's basket

Grandma: Oh, my darling child, have you brought me food?

Wolf: Err...yes?

Scene 3

Lumberjack enters

Lumberjack: Hello Mrs C Gren!

Grandmother: Oh hello dear. A wonderful day isn't it. Oh, my wonderful
granddaughter Little Red Riding Hood has come for a visit!

Lumberjack: A WOLF!

Lumberjack: GET BACK MA'AM I WILL HELP YOU!

*Narrator: The lumberjack had misunderstood the situation and picking up
his trusty axe, he readied up a mighty swing. *

Grandma: No dear, you misunderstood, this is my granddaughter,  Little
Red Riding Hood

Narrator: Clearly confused, the lumberjack paused before the wolf
started speaking

Wolf [nodding indignantly]: Yes, I am the granddaughter. And I am NOT
some wolf.

Narrator: To keep this play PG-13 we have had to censor some...vulgar
language said by the Lumberjack.

Lumberjack: I understand your vision is /censored/ but you CANNOT WALK
AROUND THINKING THAT THIS /censored for your safety/**  The /yo you
should stop swearing/ creature is NOT your granddaughter.

Wolf: No. 1) That is extremely rude. 2) I am not a dirty wolf.

Grandma: Yeah! My granddaughter is perfectly human no matter how hairy
she is.

Lumberjack: No. THAT is a wolf, and I have an axe to prove it.

Wolf: No, I am not a wolf.

Lumberjack: Yes, you are.

Wolf: No

Lumberjack: YES!

Narrator: This exchange lasted multiple hours until the sun came down.
And somehow, through the sheer power of gaslighting, the Lumberjack was
convinced that the wolf was Little Red, and they had a wonderful little
tea party.

Scene 4

Little Red walks onto stage, still clutching her phone. Visibly messy
with twigs and dust. The Wolf, Lumberjack and Grandma are all visibly
confused.

Little Red [looking up from phone]: Oh. Hi grandma.

Narrator: Somehow, through some miracle, or curse, Little Red had
survived the fall and made it to Grandma's house under a set of
impossibly impossible probabilities that had quite impossibly, happened.

Grandma: What? Grandma? But my grandchild is right there... (points
towards the Wolf)

Wolf: Er.... Yes. You are an IMPOSTER!

Lumberjack walks up to both Little Red and the Wolf. Inspect them both
clearly.

Lumberjack: I can't tell them apart. 

Grandma: Come closer you two. 

Little Red and the Wolf come closer as Grandma inspects them both.

Grandma: Hmm...

Little Red still looks at phone while the Wolf is sweating bullets.

Grandma: Hmmmm...

Everyone holds their breath in anticipation.

Grandma: I have made my decree. 

Wolf: Y-yes?

Lumberjack: What is it?

Little Red [looking up from phone]: What's happening?

Narrator: Everyone held their breath in anticipation as Grandma thought
long and hard.

Grandma: Yes. Yes. I know who the imposter is...it is...

Lumberjack: Yes?

Grandma: It is...

Wolf: Yes?

Grandma: It is...

Little Red [still looking at phone]: Hmmm??

Grandma: YOU!

/Grandma points at the Little Red. /

Little Red [looks up from phone]: What's happening?

Narrator: And Little Red was kicked out of the house. Legend says that
she is still out in the forest, wandering and searching for good Wifi.
The Wolf is now Grandma's granddaughter, and if you walk around in the
woods, you can see him dressed up in a red bonnet, travelling to
Grandma's house everyday.

/The End/
