A [[file:./Volume.org][Volume]] is a measure of the three-dimensional
[[space]] occupied by a substance or object.

In mathematics and geometry, volume is the amount of [[space]] that a
three-dimensional object occupies. It is typically measured in cubic
units (e.g., cubic meters, cubic centimeters, cubic inches).

For example, the [[file:./Volume.org][Volume]] of a cube is calculated by
multiplying its length, width, and height: \[V = l \times w \times h\]
