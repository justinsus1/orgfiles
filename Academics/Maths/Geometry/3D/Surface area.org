[[file:./Surface%20area.org][Surface area]] is the total
[[../2D/Area.org][area]] of all the surfaces or faces of a
three-dimensional object.

In simpler terms:

- [[../2D/Area.org][Area]] measures the flat space inside a 2D shape
  (like the floor of a room).
- [[file:./Surface%20area.org][Surface area]] measures the total
  [[../2D/Area.org][area]] of all the outside surfaces of a 3D object
  (like the total amount of wrapping paper needed to cover a box).
