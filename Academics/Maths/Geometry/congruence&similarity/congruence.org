#+TITLE: Congruent
#+AUTHOR: Justin Guo

Congruent figures are identical in size and shape
- Does not need to be in same position or orientation

There are 4 main proofs of congruence:
- SAS
- SSS
- AAS
- RHS

* Proofs
** SAS
SAS proof is when the angle between 2 matching sides is matching aswell

** SSS
SSS proof is when all the sides are matching

** AAS/AAcorS
AAS is when there are 2 mathcing angles and 1 matching side

** RHS
RHS is when the hypotenuse and 1 other side is matching on a right angled triangle
