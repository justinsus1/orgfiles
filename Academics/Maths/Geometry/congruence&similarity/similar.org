#+TITLE: Similarity
#+AUTHOR: Justin Guo

Similar figures have the same shape but different size

If two figures are similar then their corresponding side lengths are in the same ratio
This ratio is known as the scale factor

You can prove similarity through AAA, matching all 3 angles

* Area/Volume of similar figures/solids
To scale similar figures, multiply by the scalor, k^2
To scale similar solids, multiply by the scalor, k^3
