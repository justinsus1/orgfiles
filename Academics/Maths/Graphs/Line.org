​#Maths/Coordinates

A line can be described in 2 ways:

*** Gradient intercept form
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: gradient-intercept-form
:END:
Gradient intercept form is \(y = mx+c\) where m describes the
[[file:./Gradient.org][Gradient]], and c describes the [[./y-intercept]]

*** General form
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: general-form
:END:
General form is \(ax + by = d\) where a, b, and d are constants

- Make sure all coefficients, and constants are integers

This can be transmuted from the gradient intercept form. E.g. \[
\begin{aligned}
y = {4 \over 3} x - 4\\
3y = 4x-12\\
3y-4x=-12\\
4x-3y = 12\\
\end{aligned}
\]
