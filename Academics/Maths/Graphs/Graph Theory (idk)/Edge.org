- When 2 [[file:Vertices.org][Vertices]] are related you join them by a
  [[../Line.org][Line]] taking any form, this [[../Line.org][Line]] is
  known as an [[file:Edge.org][Edge]]

- Every [[file:Edge.org][Edge]] must have
  [[file:Vertices.org][Vertices]] at both ends [Obsidian embed requires review: ![[edge.excalidraw]]]

- If an [[file:Edge.org][Edge]] has the same vertex at both ends this is
  known as a loop [Obsidian embed requires review: ![[loopEdge.excalidraw|200]]]

- Edges are allowed to cross, but they don't “meet” unless they share a
  vertex

- If [[file:Vertices.org][Vertices]] are joined by 2 or more edges, you
  can refer to it as a double [[file:Edge.org][Edge]] or triple
  [[file:Edge.org][Edge]]
