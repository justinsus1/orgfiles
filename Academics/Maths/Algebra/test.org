** x2+bx+cx^2+bx+c

:CUSTOM_ID: x2bxcx2bxc
:END:
x2+bx+c=(x+p)(x+q)x^2+bx+c=(x+p)(x+q)

To find p,qp,q:

p+q=b\boxed{p+q=b} pq=c\boxed{pq=c}

*Connection:*

(x+p)(x+q)=x2+(p+q)x+pq(x+p)(x+q) =x^2+(p+q)x+pq

so:

p+q→bp+q\rightarrow b pq→cpq\rightarrow c

*** x2+bx+cx^2+bx+c
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: x2bxcx2bxc-1
:END:
x2+bx+c=(x+p)(x+q)\boxed{x^2+bx+c=(x+p)(x+q)}

Find p,qp,q using:

p+q=bpq=c\boxed{p+q=b} \qquad \boxed{pq=c}

*Why?*

(x+p)(x+q)=x2+(p+q)x+pq(x+p)(x+q) =x^2+(p+q)x+pq

So:

p+q→bpq→cp+q\rightarrow b \qquad pq\rightarrow c

--------------

*** ax2+bx+cax^2+bx+c
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ax2bxcax2bxc-1
:END:
*Goal:* split the middle term so that grouping works.

Find p,qp,q:

p+q=bpq=ac\boxed{p+q=b} \qquad \boxed{pq=ac}

Then:

bx→px+qxbx\rightarrow px+qx

*Process*

ax2+bx+cax^2+bx+c ↓\downarrow ax2+px+qx+cax^2+px+qx+c
↓\downarrow (ax2+px)+(qx+c)(ax^2+px)+(qx+c) ↓\downarrow

factor each group

↓\downarrow

common binomial

*** Factorise 8x2+22x+158x^2+22x+15
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: factorise-8x222x158x222x15
:END:
*Find p,qp,q:*

p+q=22p+q=22 pq=8(15)=120pq=8(15)=120 p=12, q=10\boxed{p=12,\ q=10}

*Split:*

8x2+22x+158x^2+22x+15 =8x2+12x+10x+15=8x^2+12x+10x+15

*Group:*

=(8x2+12x)+(10x+15)=(8x^2+12x)+(10x+15)

*Factor:*

=4x(2x+3)+5(2x+3)=4x(2x+3)+5(2x+3)

*Final:*

(4x+5)(2x+3)\boxed{(4x+5)(2x+3)}
