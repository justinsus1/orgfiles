** Core idea
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: core-idea
:END:
***** Factorisation of \(x^2 + bx + c\):
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: factorisation-of-x2-bx-c
:END:
\(x^2 + bx + c\) is \((x+p)(x+q)\) where \(p+q=b\) and \(pq=c\)

***** Factorisation of \(ax^2 + bx + c\):
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: factorisation-of-ax2-bx-c
:END:
is done through “splitting the middle term” where \(bx\) is split into
\((px + qx)\) where \(p+q = b\) and \(pq=ac\)

For example: \[ 
\begin{aligned}
&= 8x^2 + 22x + 15 \\
&= 8x^2 + 12x + 10x + 15 \\
&= (8x^2 + 12x) + (10x + 15) \\
&= 4x(2x + 3) + 5(2x + 3) \\
&= (4x + 5)(2x+3)
\end {aligned}
\]
