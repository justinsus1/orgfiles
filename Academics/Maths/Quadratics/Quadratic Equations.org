* What is it?
A quadratic equation is a equation that can be written as:
$ax^2+bx+c=0$

Quadratic equations can have 0, 1, or 2 solutions.

* Methods

** Null Factor Law
If ab = 0 then a = 0 or b = 0

** Completing the Square

Many quadratic expressions cannot be easily factorised, e.g. 
$x^2 + 10x - 1 = 0$ as there is no number that adds to 10 and multiplies to -1.
However, you can complete the square by rewriting it into
$(x - h)^2 = k$

In the example above you can add 26 to both sides
$x^2 + 10x + 25 = 26$
And the factorise it.

*** How do you know what number to add to both sides?
The perfect square law states that
$(x+a)^2 = x^2 + 2ax + a^2$
Half the coefficient of x, then square the result
