When drawing
[[../Maths/Graphs/Graph%20Theory%20(idk)/Graphs.org][Graphs]] in
science, what should be used?::Pencil.

Why should graphs always have a title?::The title clearly communicates
what the graph shows.

Why should you avoid shading
[[../Maths/Graphs/Graph%20Theory%20(idk)/Graphs.org][Graphs]] unless
instructed?::It can make the graph harder to read and wastes time.

Before drawing a graph, what three things should you check?::Graph type,
title, and drawing conventions (such as using pencil).

Why is having more values of the independent variable useful?::It
provides more evidence for trends and improves reliability of
conclusions.

Why should you avoid using words like “it” or “they” in scientific
explanations?::They can create ambiguity about what is being referred
to.

What should replace ambiguous pronouns in scientific writing?::The
specific object, [[../Science/Physics/Forces/Force.org][Force]],
variable, or process being discussed.

How can ambiguity reduce marks?::The examiner may not know exactly what
is being described.

Why are clear scientific diagrams important?::They communicate
information quickly and accurately.

When answering short science questions, how should your response be
described as?::A concise and precise explanation.

When a question asks for one example, how many should you usually
provide?::One, unless instructed otherwise.

What is your most common exam mistake?::Not reading the question
carefully enough.

What should you check before answering a science question?::Exactly what
the question is asking for and what is not required.

What should every scientific diagram be?::Clear, unambiguous, and only
contain required information.

What exam habit would likely gain the most marks?::Slow down briefly to
identify exactly what the question requires before starting the answer.
