Why should you write =m = 5x + 1= instead of just =5x + 1= when finding
a rule?::Because the question asks for the relationship or equation.
=5x + 1= is only the expression; =m = 5x + 1= correctly defines the
variable.

What must you do when moving a term to the other side of an
equation?::Apply the inverse operation.

If a term is added on one side of an equation and you move it to the
other side, what operation should you use?::Subtraction.

If you are unsure whether a formula is correct, what should you do?::Use
a brute-force method or work it out step-by-step.

When should you be cautious about using a fancy shortcut or
[[../Formula.org][Formula]]?::When you are not completely confident that
the [[../Formula.org][Formula]] applies.

What should you check when simplifying algebraic expressions?::That the
coefficients have been added correctly.

What should you verify before writing a final answer to an algebra
question?::That variables are defined correctly, operations have been
inverted when necessary, and like terms have been combined accurately.
