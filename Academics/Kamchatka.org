This is the East Russian Peninsula of Kamchatka, lies between the Sea of
Okhotsk and the Bering Sea. The capital of Kamchatka
Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky, is located at 53° 01' N, 158° 39' E, it is
around 9740km North of Sydney CBD, Kamchatka itself has a population of
310,000 people; despite being spread over a massive
[[file:./Maths/Geometry/2D/Area.org]], this is because it is
undeveloped and civilisation is not prominent in the
[[file:./Maths/Geometry/2D/Area.org]], the inhabitants are primarily
native Indigenous Russians; and the climate is immense to say the least;
with prolonged cold, snowy, winters and wet, cool summers. Kamchatka is
filled with volcanos, with 22 of the 129 volcanos being active to this
day as are a number of geysers and hot springs. Furthermore the
Kamchatka region primarily relies on fishing as its economic activity,
as most other options for a rural civilisation such as Kamchatka are
limited because of the severe climate (Kamchatka Peninsula | peninsula,
Russia, 2019).

On July 30, 2025 ​at 11:24:52 Kamchatka Time, moment magnitude 8.8
Earthquake struck off the eastern coast of the Kamchatka Peninsula in
Russia (Xu, Wang and Ren, 2025). The Earthquake occurred at
52.495°N  160.240°E, and at a depth of 35km, this makes it one of the
strongest earthquakes recorded since 1900 (Unesco.org, 2025; Usgs.gov,
2025). The pacific plate converges with the Okhotsk microplate and
descends beneath the microplate; this causes rocks near the surface to
push upward and create an earthquake.

Several pacific countries were sent warnings and guides and Russia
experienced a tsunami of 19m high, wave aptitudes in the pacific region
ranged from 0.5-1.5m (Science On a Sphere, 2025). Multiple airports and
railways were forced to close as a result. Material and service
disruptions also occurred during the Earthquake as the city of
Pertopavlovsk-Kamchatsky experienced a number of power outages, many
school and hospitals developed cracks. However the Kuril islands
experienced the most severe consequences. In Severo-Kurilsk, all 106
apartment buildings and even the town's social and utility facilities
were damaged. The town was flooded by tsunami water that reached up to
600m inland; the flood damaged numerous buildings, as they were swept
away (Noor et al., 2026).

Across Asia, numerous safety measure were put in place. Russia declared
a state of emergency after the waves struck. The Japan Meteorological
Agency issued widespread tsunami warnings and approximately ~2,000,000
Japanese people were advised to evacuate (Noor et al., 2026); an
abundance of power plants were shut down and evacuated as a precaution
(Unesco.org, 2025). Furthermore, tsunami alerts were also issued in
China, the Philippines, Indonesia, New Zealand, Vietnam, and Malaysia.
