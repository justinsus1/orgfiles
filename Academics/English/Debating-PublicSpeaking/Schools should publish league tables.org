Imagine where you have no insight into a school's quality of education
or teachers. This is the world of the opposition, the opposition has
taken the stance that schools should not publish league tables. However
I firmly believe that school should publish league tables, I have two
mechanisms for this.

To begin with, people deserve information, parents should have a right
to know the different qualities of the school before sending their own
child. For example, if your child wishes to be academically strong you
would probably enroll them in Sydney Grammar School because it is
academically strong, however if you didn't know that Sydney Grammar
School is academically strong you could've enrolled them into King's
School, a school is very strong at sports. Without information you would
have nothing to base your choices on. This would make it much more
stressful for the parents to choose a school and make it hard to choose
a school that will fit the child's needs.

Additionally, high performing school and teachers will be recognised for
their excellence or discipline. Recognising high performance teachers
will give great teachers a morale boost, after seeing the success of
their school/class, it will give great teachers the confidence and
morale to keep teaching, students having a good or bad teacher can
significantly impact their grades. Below average students having the
knowledge that their school is high performing can impact below average
students as knowing that the standard is high, they will be motivated
and pushed to perform at a higher level. Giving the recognition to great
school and teachers that deserve it will improve a school's academic
ability.

Therefore, schools should publish League tables because, it will boost
literacy and help parents choose the right schools for their child.
