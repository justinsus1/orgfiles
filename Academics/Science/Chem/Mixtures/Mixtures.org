#+title: Mixtures
#+author: Justin Guo

A mixture is any 2 or more substances mixed together

Mixtures can be:
- Homogeneous: equal composition throughout
- Heterogeneous: different composition throughout

A general way to determine if a substance is a mixture is 
the difficulty of seperation of the substances within it

Main types of mixtures:
- Homogeneous
  - Solutions
  - Colloids
- Heterogeneous
  - Suspensions
