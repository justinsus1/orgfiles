#+title: Solutions
#+author: Justin Guo

When a substance dissolves in another they become a solution.

A solution is made of 2 parts.
- Solvent
  The substance that substances dissolve in

- Solute
  The substance that dissolves in a solvent

Properties:
- A solution with water as a solvent is known as a aqueous solution
- A solution is always homogeneous (same composition throughout)
