​#Science/Biology ## Utilise MRS C GREN

Movement: Can move Respiration: Conversion of fuel to chemical energy
Sensitivity: Responds to stimulus Control: Can maintain internal
conditions Growth: Can grow Reproduction: Can reproduce Excretion: Gets
rid of waste Nutrition: Intaking food to survive

What does MRS C GREN stand for?::Movement, respiration, sensitivity,
control, growth, reproduction, excretion, and nutrition

What does the M stand for in MRS C GREN and what does it mean?::The M
stands for movement, this can include plants growing direction of the
sun, or fungi using their roots to gather food What does the R stand for
in the first half MRS C GREN and what does it mean?::The R stands for
respiration, this is the conversion of fuel to chemical energy What does
the S stand for in MRS C GREN and what does it mean?:: The S stands for
Sensitivity, this means if it respond to any stimulus, for example an
animal responds by presumably running away if you hurt it What does the
C stand for in MRS C GREN and what does it mean?::The C stands for
control, this means can it perform homeostasis; if it can maintain
internal conditions such as body heat, or water levels What does the G
stand for in MRS C GREN and what does it mean?::The G stands for growth,
this means if it can grow What does the R stand for in second half MRS C
GREN and what does it mean?::In the second half, the R stands for
reproduction, if it can reproduce What does the E stand for in MRS C
GREN and what does it mean?::The E stands for excretion, if it can
excrete waste from its body What does the N stand for in MRS C GREN and
what does it mean?::The N stands for nutrition, if it intakes nutrition
to survive
