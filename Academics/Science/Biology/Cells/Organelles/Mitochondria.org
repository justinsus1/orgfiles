** Respiration
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: respiration
:END:
*** Animalia
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: animalia
:END:
Aerobic respiration for animalia is Glucose + O2 (dioxide - 2 oxygen) ->
CO2 (carbon dioxide) + Water + ATP (chemical energy)

Anaerobic respiration for animalia is Glucose -> ATP (chemical energy) +
lactic acid (makes muscles stiffness) - O2 (dioxide - 2 oxygen) helps
break down lactic acid into more ATP, CO2 and Water
