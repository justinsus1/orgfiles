​#Science

An [[file:./Electrons.org][Electrons]] is a stable, negatively charged
elementary particle that orbits the
[[file:./Nucleus-physics.org][Nucleus-physics]] of an atom. It carries a
charge of −1 and has negligible mass compared to
[[file:./Protons.org][Protons]] and [[file:./Neutrons.org][Neutrons]].

In [[file:./Atoms.org][Atoms]], [[file:./Electrons.org][Electrons]] occupy
the space around the [[file:./Nucleus-physics.org][Nucleus-physics]] in
regions called orbitals (or electron clouds).
