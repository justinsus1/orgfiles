​#Science

[[file:./Protons.org][Protons]] are positively charged subatomic particle
found in the [[file:./Nucleus-physics.org][Nucleus-physics]] of an atom,
made of two up quarks and one down quark. It carries a charge of +1, and
its count in the [[./file:Nucleus-physics.org][Nucleus-physics]] defines
what element an atom is --- for example, any atom with 6 protons is
carbon. [[file:./Protons.org][Protons]] are stable (they don't decay under
normal conditions) and, together with [[file:./Neutrons.org][Neutrons]],
make up virtually all of an atom's [[./mass]]
