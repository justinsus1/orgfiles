​#Science

A neutron is a neutrally charged subatomic particle found in the atomic
[[file:./Nucleus-physics.org][Nucleus-physics]], made of one up quark and
two down quarks. It helps stabilise the
[[file:./Nucleus-physics.org][Nucleus-physics]] by counteracting proton
repulsion, determines an element's isotope. Free neutrons are
unstable, decaying in about 10 minutes.
