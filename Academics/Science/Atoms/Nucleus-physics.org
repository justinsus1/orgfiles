​#Science

A [[file:Nucleus-physics.org][Nucleus-physics]] is the dense, central
part
of [[file:Atoms.org][Atoms]] where [[obsidian://open?file=Science%2FAtoms%2FProtons.md][Protons]] and [[obsidian://open?file=Science%2FAtoms%2FNeutrons.md][Neutrons]] are
found. It is the core of the atom, and the number
of [[obsidian://open?file=Science%2FAtoms%2FProtons.md][Protons]] in
the [[obsidian://open?file=Science%2FAtoms%2FNucleus.md][Nucleus]] defines
the element. The nucleus contains all the mass of the atom. The
[[file:Nucleus-physics.org][Nucleus-physics]] is held together by the
strong nuclear [[../Physics/Forces/Force.org][Force]] of
[[file:Neutrons.org][Neutrons]].
