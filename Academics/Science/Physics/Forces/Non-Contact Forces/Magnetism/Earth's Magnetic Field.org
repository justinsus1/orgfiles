- The Earth is surrounded by a magnetic field
- The Earth is the only rocky planet in our solar system to have a
  magnetic field

The movement of liquid iron in Earth's outer core creates the magnetic
field

[[file:earthLayers.jpg]] [[file:earthMagneticField.png]]

The poles can reverse (last happened 780,000 years ago)

*** Importance:
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: importance
:END:
The magnetic fields shield Earth from harmful radiation and particles
(mainly from the Sun). Without the magnetic field radiation could
destroy Earth's atmosphere.

*** Auroras:
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: auroras
:END:
[[file:aurora.jpg]] Auroras are caused by charged [[particles]]
deflecting off of the
[[file:Earth's%20Magnetic%20Field.org][Earth's Magnetic Field]]. When
they deflect they deflect towards the magnetic poles and hit the
atmosphere, this results in vibrant rays/curtains across the sky.

*** Animals utilising the magnetic field:
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: animals-utilising-the-magnetic-field
:END:
Many different animals use the Earth's magnetic field to navigate, much
like how humans use compasses. However scientists have yet to figure out
how they achieve this
