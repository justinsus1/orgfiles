​#Science/Physics/Forces

Electrostatic [[../Force.org][force]] refers to the
[[../Force.org][force]] between stationary electric charges.

*** Charges
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: charges
:END:
- When there is more [[../../../Atoms/Electrons.org][Electrons]] than
  [[../../../Atoms/Protons.org][Protons]] an atom is negatively charged
- When there is more [[../../../Atoms/Protons.org][Protons]] than
  [[../../../Atoms/Electrons.org][Electrons]] an atom is positively
  charged
- When the amount of [[../../../Atoms/Electrons.org][Electrons]] to
  [[../../../Atoms/Protons.org][Protons]] are equal the atom is
  neutrally charged
